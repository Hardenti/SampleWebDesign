Designed to strengthen nuclear security culture through meaningful campus engagement.

Framework

Program criteria aligned with nuclear security, academic engagement, and mission readiness.

Core program requirements

  • Campus outreach strategy designed to strengthen understanding of nuclear security topics across educational institutions.
  • Clear communication structure for stakeholders, college partners, faculty, and student audiences.
  • Educational content and engagement activities that promote responsible awareness and informed participation.
  • Partnership development with universities, research centers, and technical organizations.
  • Metrics and reporting frameworks that track participation, outreach engagement, and program impact.
  • Secure and accessible digital experience designed for clarity, trust, and professional communication.

Priority capabilities

  • Student engagement opportunities, internships, and workforce development pathways.
  • Content strategy tailored for academic, technical, and public-facing audiences.
  • Research coordination and cross-institution collaboration opportunities.
  • Security awareness programming aligned with policy, safeguards, and mission priorities.
  • Integration of analytics to guide outreach decisions and program growth.
  • Strong governance, compliance awareness, and credibility in a highly sensitive field.
